Whispering Pines Restoration & Blueprint Guide

Floor Hierarchy, Lineage Indentation, Task Transfers & Safety Backups
🌳 1. Left Sidebar Floor Lineage & Indented Rooms
The left sidebar is organized as an architectural tree. Each major floor header (e.g. Level 3: Tower & Roof, Level 2: Upper Guest Suites, Level 1: Main Living, Level 0: Sub-Cellar Foundation, and Grounds & Outbuildings) acts as the parent folder.

Sub-rooms are deeply indented by half an inch with solid tree branch connectors (├── and └──) so you can clearly see room lineage. You can collapse or expand floors individually by clicking their header, or use the ▼ ALL and ▲ ALL buttons at the top of the sidebar.
💡 Drag & Drop Reordering: Click and drag any room card up or down to reorder within a floor, or drag it into another floor level. Hold Ctrl / Alt while dragging to duplicate the entire room!
✏️ 2. Renaming Main Floor Level Titles
You can rename any main floor header! Simply double-click directly on the floor name to edit it inline, or click the ✏️ Rename Label button to change its title, icon, or elevation marker.
✂️ 3. Dragging, Moving & Copying Tasks Between Rooms
You have two fast ways to relocate or copy tasks:
  • Click & Drag: Grab the ⋮⋮ Drag handle on any task card and drag it onto any room card in the left sidebar! It will instantly move the task into that room. Hold Ctrl / Alt while dragging to copy it instead.
  • Transfer Buttons: Click ✂️ Move Room or 📋 Copy Task directly on the task card to choose a destination from a dropdown.
  • Reorder Tasks: Drag a task card above or below other tasks in the same room to reorder your work sequence.
💡 Undo Support: If you accidentally move or delete a task or room, click the ↩️ Undo button on the top toolbar anytime.
📸 4. Phased Restoration Journals (Before / During / After)
Under the 📸 Photos & Logs tab, each room features three distinct chronological phases: Before (hazard assessment & original condition), During (rough-in framing, electrical, plumbing), and After (finished trim, paint, staged reveal). You can attach photos, record field notes, and document contractor work orders.
💾 5. 5-Minute Auto-Backups & 30-Minute Safe Downloads
Your project is continuously protected:
  • Every 5 minutes: The system automatically writes a rolling snapshot to your browser's persistent storage.
  • Every 30 minutes: A friendly notification pops up with a 1-click button to download an offline .json project file to your computer.
  • You can also click the glowing green 💾 Auto-Backup Active badge in the top toolbar anytime to download an immediate backup.
