Bench Hack #1 🪚

Zero-Clearance Miter Throat for Antique Moldings

Clamp a 1/4-inch piece of Baltic birch plywood to your sliding miter saw table and cut through at 0° and 45°. Prevents brittle 116-year-old fir cove moldings from chipping out on the bottom face.

Bench Hack #2 ⚡

Electrolytic De-Rusting of 1910 Mortise Locks

Washing soda + 12V trickle charger converts red rust (Fe2O3) to black magnetite (Fe3O4) overnight without dissolving delicate brass internal tumbler springs or patent date stampings.

Bench Hack #3 🧱

Breathable Lime Wash for Stone Cellar Retaining

Mix hydrated lime putty with salt and water for a brilliant white cellar wash. Naturally antimicrobial, resists mold, and allows damp 1910 un-mortared stone foundation walls to transpire moisture safely.

Bench Hack #4 🪵

True Oak Wedge Shimming During Jacking

Always use dry white oak wedges under temporary screw-jack cribbing. Soft pine shims compress under 60 tons of dead weight, causing frame settling within hours after the hydraulic jacks are bled.
