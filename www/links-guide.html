1. What Are Subtask Links?

In large-scale historic renovations or complex project management, tasks rarely exist in isolation. A task on the 1st Floor (such as plastering the Grand Parlor ceiling) frequently depends on a task in the Attic / Roof (such as sealing flashing around the cupola), or a mechanical rough-in in the Basement.

The Subtask Linker bridges the gap between independent Main Task folders (floors, levels, phases, or divisions). It allows any subtask card to maintain an active reference to one or more subtasks in other folders, complete with dependency notes and real-time status tracking.

Bidirectional Dependency Relationship
MAIN TASK: Level 1
Guest Parlor & Hearth
🔗 Attic Slate Roofing
◀━━━━▶ BIDIRECTIONAL LINK (Syncs notes & statuses)
MAIN TASK: Level 3
Victorian Slate Roofing
🔗 Parlor & Hearth
💡 Automatic Reciprocity: When you link Room A to Room B, the system automatically writes the reciprocal link in Room B pointing back to Room A! You never have to duplicate links manually.

2. Step-by-Step: How to Link Subtasks

Follow these 3 quick steps from either the Projects & Tasks sidebar or the Renovations workspace tab:

01

🔗 Click "LINK" on Any Card

Every subtask card features a violet 🔗 LINK action button. Click it to open the Subtask Link Manager modal for that specific task.

02

🎯 Select Target Folder & Subtask

Choose which Main Task folder (e.g. Level 3: Tower & Roof) and which Subtask (e.g. Cupola Flashing) you want to link. The selector prevents duplicate links.

03

📝 Add Note & Save

Type a dependency note (e.g. "Must complete copper flashing before parlor plaster"), then click 🔗 Link Subtasks. The connection is instantly active!

📌 Persistent Storage: All subtask links are saved directly to your browser's persistent storage, included in the SAVE file, and synchronized across cloud saves.
🚀

3. Instant 1-Click Navigation (Jumping)

Once subtasks are linked, a clickable violet badge chip appears right inside the subtask's detail area and card view:

Example Linked Chip on a Task Card:
🔗 Victorian Slate Roofing & Cupola DONE (Level 3: Tower & Roof)
Try clicking the chip above to see what happens!

What happens when you click a linked chip?

  • The sidebar automatically expands the destination Main Task folder (if collapsed).
  • The view smoothly scrolls directly to the linked subtask.
  • The destination subtask flashes with an emerald/violet highlight ring to catch your eye immediately.

4. Subtask Links vs. Custom Buttons

In the Projects & Tasks Settings (opened via the ⚙️ SETTINGS button), you will notice both Subtask Links and Custom Buttons. Here is how they compare:

Feature 🔗 Subtask Links (Cross-Folder) 🎛️ Custom Action Buttons
Primary Purpose Track construction dependencies & multi-task workflows between rooms. Provide custom 1-click launcher buttons on subtask headers.
Directionality Bidirectional — automatically updates both linked rooms. Targeted — triggers an action when clicked.
Supported Targets Any Subtask across any Main Task folder. Subtasks, Workspace Tabs (Blueprints, Starting Point, Timeline), or External URLs.
Dependency Notes Yes — custom notes explaining prerequisite requirements. Optional button labels and color accents.
Where Configured Directly on any card via the 🔗 LINK button. Inside ⚙️ SETTINGS → Custom Subtask Buttons tab.

5. Real-World Renovation Scenarios

Here are practical scenarios from the 1910 Queen Anne Manor restoration demonstrating when to link subtasks:

Scenario A: Roof Flashing before Ceiling Plaster Weatherproof Dependency

Never apply lime plaster or prime historic parlor ceilings until slate valley flashing and gutter transitions in the Roof Main Task folder are signed off as watertight.

Scenario B: Foundation Jacks & 2nd Floor Door Sticking Structural Dependency

Before planning, rehanging, or trimming sticking doors in the 2nd Floor Bedrooms, verify that the 20-ton screw jacks in the Basement Structural Ledger have finished settling.

Scenario C: Architectural Salvage Inventory to Master Suite Material Reuse Dependency

Link antique tiger-oak pocket doors salvaged from the ground floor to the Master Suite installation checklist so craftsmen know where materials are staged.

6. Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How many subtasks can I link together?
There is no limit! You can link a single subtask to multiple rooms across different floors. Each connection is displayed with its own distinct chip and note.
Q: How do I delete or remove a link?
Click the 🔗 LINK button on the subtask card to open the manager. Scroll to the "Active Subtask Links" section and click the red 🗑️ Remove button next to the link. Removing it automatically cleans up both sides!
Q: Do links get deleted if I rename a folder or room?
No! Links use unique internal room keys, so you can freely rename room titles, change emojis, or re-order folders without breaking your dependency links.
Q: Are links included when I click "SAVE"?
Yes! All links, notes, and timestamps are bundled into the export state, saved to your local PC, and synced across all browser tabs and cloud polling sessions.
